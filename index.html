So, What Is A Revolution?

ANSWER

Revolution Isn't Just A Group Of People Protesting In A Particular Place All Of A Sudden, But A Series Of Gradual Changes That Affects The Society Of A Specific Region Or Belief To Change, By Many Ordinary People Belonging From Different Groups.

WHY DID THE REVOLUTION HAPPEN?

PEOPLE OF FRANCE WEREN'T SATISFIED WITH THE SOCIAL , POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC ORDER.

CAUSE 01

SOCIAL ORDER

THE FRENCH SOCIETY WAS DIVIDED INTO THREE ESTATES .

FIRST ESTATE CLERGY
SECOND ESTATE NOBLES
THIRD ESTATE COMMONERS Peasants • Businesses • Court Officials / Lawyers

PEASANTS → Those Who Work On The Field.

COURT OFFICIALS → Lawyers, etc.

THE REIGN OF TERROR

MAXIMILIEN ROBESPIERRE'S RULE OF FEAR

Robespierre and the Jacobins exercised power during a period of severe political repression.

What Were The Changes During The Reign Of Terror?

ANSWER
  • Robespierre administered the country through fear and strict control.
  • The measures became so harsh that even his followers demanded moderation.
  • Bread and meat were rationed.
  • During de-Christianisation, some churches and religious symbols were repurposed, removed, or used for secular purposes.

WHAT'S A BARRACK?

KEY TERM

Why Did The Reign Of Terror End?

ANSWER

Maximilien Robespierre, leader of the Jacobin Club and the dominant political figure of the period, was charged with treason and later guillotined.

DIRECTORY RULE

An executive system led by five members who governed France from 1795 to 1799.

KEY POINT 📌

The five-member Directory faced political instability, which helped create conditions for Napoleon's rise to power.

RISE OF NAPOLEON

WHO WAS NAPOLEON BONAPARTE?

Napoleon Bonaparte was a military leader who gained power in France and became Emperor of France in 1804.

1804 Napoleon became Emperor of France
IMAGE NAPOLEON BONAPARTE
QUESTION

What Changes Did Napoleon Bring?

ANSWER
  • He spread ideas associated with liberty and equality across France and Europe.
  • He introduced laws and institutions that weakened inherited privilege and promoted equality before the law.
  • He conquered or dominated many European territories, including areas of Austria, Prussia, and Italy.
  • His invasion of Russia in 1812 failed and became a major turning point.
THE FATAL MISTAKE
